Our Mission

Our mission is to make educational resources easier to access and help students prepare more effectively for their examinations.

How It Works

Students will be able to search for past papers by school, subject, grade and year. Users will also be able to submit papers for review before they become publicly available.

PastPaper Hub is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with any school unless explicitly stated.

I created PastPaper Hub using HTML to structure the website, CSS to design its appearance, and JavaScript to make it interactive. I integrated Supabase to store past-paper information and manage uploaded files. The website allows learners to search for past papers by school, subject, grade, and year. Uploaded papers must be approved by an administrator before they become available to the public.
